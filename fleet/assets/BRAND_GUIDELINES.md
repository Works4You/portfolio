## 1. Brand Color Palette
- **Solar Amber:** `#FF8A00` (RGB: 255, 138, 0)
- **Hyper Magenta:** `#FF1E6A` (RGB: 255, 30, 106)
- **Royal Purple:** `#7C3AED` (RGB: 124, 58, 237)
- **Electric Cyan:** `#06B6D4` (RGB: 6, 182, 212)
- **Deep Obsidian (Dark Text / BG):** `#0F172A` / `#0B0F19`
- **Pure Cloud White:** `#FFFFFF`

---

## 2. Typographic Anatomy
- **'Works':** Custom geometric neo-grotesque with 30° precision chamfered stencil cuts on the 'W', 'k', and 's'.
- **'4you':** Continuous 3-stop chromatic energy gradient flowing from Solar Amber through Hyper Magenta into Royal Purple.
- **'AI':** Luminous Electric Cyan capsule / neural pill.
- **Emblem:** 3D Isometric Segmented Möbius Loop with glowing cyan energy seams.
